Before AI: The Traditional Online Sales Flow

In many industrial B2B businesses, the traditional sales flow still looks like this: A customer visits the website, webstore, product page, ad landing page, or social media page. The customer has a question but only sees a form, phone number, or WhatsApp link. The customer sends a short enquiry with incomplete details.

The message waits until someone checks it. A salesperson replies later and asks for more information. The customer may reply slowly or may already be speaking to another supplier. The salesperson searches for product information, pricing, availability, or technical details.

The conversation continues with repeated back-and-forth. This process is common, but it is not scalable. It depends heavily on manpower, working hours, individual sales experience, and manual follow-up. For businesses receiving many online enquiries, this creates missed opportunities, slow response, inconsistent answers, and wasted sales time.
