What Antbuildz AI Agent Should Not Do

A good AI Agent should be powerful, but it should also stay within proper business rules. Antbuildz AI Agent should not invent product availability, pricing, technical specifications, or agreement terms if the information is not available in the approved knowledge base. It should not confirm stock, final pricing, service diagnosis, or custom terms unless the business has configured the AI Agent to do so.

For complex technical cases, special pricing, customised projects, safety-sensitive situations, or information outside the configured scope, the AI Agent should collect details and guide the customer to the right next step. This balance is important. The goal is to help the AI Agent handle a large portion of common early-stage sales conversations while keeping the business in control.
