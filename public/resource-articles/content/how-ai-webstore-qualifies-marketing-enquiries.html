What Antbuildz AI Agent Should Not Do

A good AI Agent should help marketing conversion, but it should still follow business rules.

Antbuildz AI Agent should not invent product specifications, confirm final stock unless inventory data is reliable, promise delivery dates without business confirmation, decide special pricing without approved rules, guess spare part compatibility when data is unclear, make legal or contractual commitments outside approved terms, and provide unsafe technical diagnosis for complex cases. If information is unclear, the AI Agent should ask follow-up questions or prepare the enquiry for human review.

This protects customer trust while still improving campaign conversion.
