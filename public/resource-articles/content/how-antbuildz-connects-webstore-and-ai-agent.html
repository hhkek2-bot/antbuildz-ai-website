What Antbuildz Should Not Overpromise

A strong digital sales ecosystem should still stay within business rules.

The AI Agent should not invent product specifications, guess spare part compatibility when data is unclear, confirm final stock unless inventory data is reliable, promise delivery dates without business confirmation, decide special pricing without approved rules, or make legal commitments outside approved terms. If information is unclear, the AI Agent should ask follow-up questions or prepare the enquiry for human review. This balance is important.

The system should help automate and qualify the early-stage sales process, while keeping final business judgement under the company’s control.
